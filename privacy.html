Privacy Notice

Health & Nutrition Check

Version 2026-09-11. Last updated 11 September 2026.

This notice explains what information we collect when you use the Health & Nutrition Check, why we collect it, how long we keep it and your rights. Please read it before you create an account.

1. Who we are

Aishwarya Intl Ltd ("Aishwarya International", "we", "us") is the data controller. We are an independent Amway Business Owner based in Sunbury-on-Thames, United Kingdom.

Contact us about anything in this notice at aishwaryaintl@outlook.com.

2. What we collect

TypeDetails
Account detailsName, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), and your password in scrambled (hashed) form. We never see or store your actual password.
Body measurementsAge, sex, height, weight and the BMI we calculate from them.
Lifestyle answersExercise, fruit and vegetable intake, sleep, stress and whether you smoke.
Health informationThe health areas you tick (for example diabetes or asthma), whether you take prescription medicine or a blood thinner, whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, and fish or shellfish allergies. This is "special category" data under UK data protection law.
Your choicesYour consent choices, the date you gave them, the version of this notice you agreed to, and whether you asked us to contact you.

We don't collect the names of any medicines, your date of birth, your address or payment details. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

What we doLegal basis (UK GDPR)
Create your account and show your resultsYour consent (Article 6(1)(a)). For health information, your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)).
Contact you about your results or Nutrilite productsYour consent, given by ticking the contact box when you register or asking for a call on the results page.

Your results are produced automatically from your answers, using rules based on a published book. They are general educational suggestions. They don't have any legal effect on you, and nobody makes decisions about you from them.

4. Who can see your information

Only Aishwarya International (Girish BP and anyone he authorises to follow up with you) can see your information. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

We use these service providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail) stores your account and answers and sends us a daily summary email of new sign-ups.
  • Microsoft (Outlook.com) holds the inbox that receives that daily summary.
  • GitHub hosts the web pages. Your answers are sent straight to Google and are not stored by GitHub.

If you choose to buy Nutrilite products, you will place your order with Amway, and Amway's own privacy notice applies to that order.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Google and Microsoft may process data in the United States or other countries. When they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement and Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for 24 months after your last activity (registering, completing a check or asking for a call). After that it is deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

7. Your rights

  • Withdraw consent at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew.
  • Delete your data. Use "Delete my data" when logged in, or email us.
  • Access a copy of your information.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Portability: get your information in a common format.
  • Stop contact: reply "stop" to any message, or email us.

Email aishwaryaintl@outlook.com to use any of these rights. We will reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

8. Keeping it safe

The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. The data sits in a single Google account protected by 2-step verification, and access is limited to Aishwarya International.

9. Age

The Health & Nutrition Check is only for people aged 18 or over.

10. Not medical advice

The check is for education. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease and does not replace advice from your GP, pharmacist or specialist. Keep taking any medicine you are prescribed.

11. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll update the version date at the top. If a change affects how we use your health information, we'll ask for your consent again.
